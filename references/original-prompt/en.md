# XXD Shop 005 · Original brief (English reading translation)

> Reading translation only. The canonical runtime prompt is [zh-CN.md](zh-CN.md).

Generate a bright, clean, orderly e-commerce scene image with a lived-in quality for the current beauty product.

If the user provides a white-background or real product photo, strictly keep the original product's shape, proportions, packaging colour, logo, labels, bottle material and key identifying features, without redesigning the product; only rebuild light, shadow, reflection and spatial relationships consistent with the new environment so the product exists in the scene truly and naturally.

The first requirement is "the subject is clear at a glance". The product sits in the most definite, brightest and most complete visual position, with a clean outline and bottle text as legible as possible, never disturbed by props, shadows, reflections or a complex background.

Use high-key, bright indoor natural light. The space is fully lit by soft diffuse light, with no large black areas, heavy vignettes, hard light-dark cuts or oppressive shadows. Shadows only express the product's footing and volume and should be light, soft and clean; white, cream, light wood, light grey and soft brand colours build layers through slight differences in value and material, not through dark contrast.

The background stays simple, orderly and breathing. Props are actively restrained: choose only a few objects matching the product's character, such as a neat storage cabinet, a simple tray, a glass jar, a mirror frame, folded fabric, a metal shelf, flowers or books. Keep prop outlines simple and their horizontal, vertical and layered relationships clear; no random scattering, no messy traces of daily life.

The product may rest naturally on a light ceramic dish, a stone tray, a clean tabletop or a simple storage area, so the supporting surface is stable, complete and orderly. Background objects form spatial layers but always recede; they do not surround the product or compete with it.

Composition leans stable, comfortable and clear; it may be centred or slightly off-centre, but overall balanced. Avoid too many diagonals, complex occlusion, blurred foreground objects, lots of scattered small items and a deliberately "snapped carelessly" feel. The image is like a beauty photo naturally taken in the home of someone with great taste who keeps it very tidy.

Colour is light, soft and positive overall; white and light areas stay clear, not grey, not yellow, not dirty. The product's own colour may become the image's main colour memory, with the rest of the environment quietly supporting it.

Final effect: a clear subject, a simple background, a bright space, orderly props, soft layering, a real-camera look, clean and soothing without an excessive creamy filter — suitable for lifestyle posts, beauty recommendations and e-commerce scene images.

Avoid: large dark areas, black backgrounds, strong side light, heavy shadows, vignettes, cluttered props, random scattering, complex occlusion, a messy foreground, overly vintage filters, too much decoration, and a background with too much information.
